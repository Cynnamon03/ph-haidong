Who is Francis Pisa?

Meet Francis Pisa, a distinguished figure in the realm of martial arts, embodying a legacy of excellence and versatility. As the Philippine Headmaster of Haidong Gumdo and a member of Kukkiwon, Francis's martial arts journey is marked by a relentless pursuit of mastery across various disciplines. Formerly a prominent member of the Philippine Pencak Silat national team and a dedicated practitioner of Kyukushin, Francis's dedication to martial arts knows no bounds.

In addition to his achievements in traditional martial arts, Francis has ventured into the realm of mixed martial arts (MMA), competing in the renowned URCC (Universal Reality Combat Championship) in the Philippines. In a remarkable display of skill and determination, Francis emerged victorious in his bout, defeating his opponent in less than a minute of the fight—an impressive feat that underscores his prowess and adaptability in combat sports.

"Francis's accolades extend beyond the realm of MMA, as he is also a six-time International open medalist and a European champion in Karate."

His illustrious career includes competing in prestigious events such as the Kyokushinkaikan 7th World Open Karate Tournament in November 1999 where he finished 48 out of 192 participants, showcasing his skills on a global stage.

Today, as the Philippine Headmaster of Haidong Gumdo, Francis continues to inspire and empower martial artists of all levels, guiding them on their own paths of discovery and mastery. His journey is a testament to the transformative power of martial arts—a journey marked by dedication, perseverance, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Furthermore, Francis Pisa's influence extends globally as he shares his expertise and passion for martial arts in Germany, Ireland, the United States, and currently in Australia. Through his teachings and mentorship, Francis continues to impact martial artists around the world, fostering a community of growth, resilience, and martial arts excellence.
